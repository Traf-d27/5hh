## 🔄 Architectural Workflow: The KlimaTwin Paradigm

ECHOTECH adapts the closed-loop workflow inspired by the **KlimaTwin** repository:

$$\text{SENSE} \longrightarrow \text{ANALYZE} \longrightarrow \text{DETECT} \longrightarrow \text{RECOMMEND} \longrightarrow \text{ACT} \longrightarrow \text{VERIFY} \longrightarrow \text{LEARN}$$

1. **SENSE:** Multi-source data ingestion (CSV upload, manual operator entry, and physics-calibrated synthetic streams) with 4 strict data classifications.
2. **ANALYZE:** Rolling averages, diurnal insolation baselines, wastewater mass balances, and CEA carbon accounting.
3. **DETECT:** Explainable statistical anomaly detection across Solar, Electricity, Water, and Waste without premature hardware accusations.
4. **RECOMMEND:** Actionable sustainability nudges with explicit assumptions, target reduction percentages, and financial ROI (₹ INR).
5. **ACT:** 1-click conversion from anomaly or recommendation into an assigned campus initiative.
6. **VERIFY:** Empirical IPMVP Option B before-and-after meter logging to separate **Potential Savings** from **Verified Realized Savings**.
7. **LEARN:** Dynamic feedback loops updating baseline models and recalibrating future projections.

## 🏷️ Multi-Tier Data Classifications (Integrity Rules)

To prevent misrepresenting demonstration data to judges, ECHOTECH enforces 4 strict classifications that are **never silently combined**:

1. **`OFFICIAL_REFERENCE` (Official Published Reference):** Fixed benchmarks cited directly from Presidency University portals (e.g. 800 kWh/day Solar, 300,000 L/day STP, 120-150 kg/day Waste).
2. **`SYNTHETIC_DEMO` (Synthetic Demonstration Data):** Physics-calibrated simulated streams demonstrating load curves, weather impacts, and anomaly scenarios until live sensors are linked.
3. **`IMPORTED_MEASUREMENT` (Imported Campus Measurement):** Field telemetry uploaded via CSV spreadsheets or logged via the manual operator entry form.
4. **`LIVE_SENSOR` (Live Sensor Measurement):** Reserved for direct IoT inverter/sub-meter integrations.

- **Problem Statement:** Transforming Presidency University's published sustainability commitments into an actionable, empirical management loop.
- **Architecture:** Walk through the KlimaTwin `SENSE → ANALYZE → DETECT → RECOMMEND → ACT → VERIFY → LEARN` pipeline.
- **Campus Specificity:** Point out the **Central Administration Building rooftop solar array**, the **300 kL SBR STP**, and the **cafeteria vermicomposting pits**.

### Round 2 — 20 Marks (Implementation Progress & Innovation)
- **Flagship Solar Intelligence:** Show the **diurnal solar generation curve vs campus demand**. Demonstrate how solar peak hours (11:00-14:00) substitute expensive BESCOM grid imports.
- **Explainable Anomaly Trigger:** Navigate to **Inefficiency Alerts**. Inspect the **Rooftop Solar Soiling Anomaly** (output dropped 38% below clear-sky baseline due to dust) and the **Central Lawn Irrigation Pipe Rupture** (+30% water surge).
- **Audit Flag Innovation:** Click the **Audit Flag** in the navbar to show the reconciliation of the 800 kWh/day published reference vs the unverified published monthly savings figure.

### Round 3 — 60 Marks (Full Working Solution, ROI & Verification)
- **Sustainability Nudges:** Show the **Recommendations** view with target reduction percentages, capex payback periods in months, and avoided metric tons of $CO_2e$ (CEA Factor: 0.716 kg $CO_2e$/kWh).
- **Closed-Loop Action Tracker:** Convert an anomaly into an action with 1 click. Move an action from *In Progress* to *Resolved*.
- **Impact Verification Ledger:** Open the **Impact Verification** page to demonstrate the IPMVP Option B ledger. Show the clear separation between **Identified Potential Savings** and **Verified Realized Savings** backed by post-intervention meter logs.
- **Data Ingestion:** Demonstrate the **Data Management** page by downloading a sample CSV template and testing the manual telemetry entry form.
